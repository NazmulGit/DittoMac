## DittoMac 1.2.0: screenshots, locked notes, cloud sync

### Screenshots, so you don't need Lightshot
- **⌃⇧4** area · **⌃⇧5** window · **⌃⇧3** full screen (configurable). Drag to select, move and resize, then annotate:
  pen, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, marker, text, numbered steps, pixelate, blur, colour picker, undo/redo.
- Copy, save (PNG/JPG/BMP), print, **pin to screen**, **upload and share by link**, share to X, Facebook, Pinterest,
  VK, Reddit, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram or email, and search for similar images.
- **Beautify:** 18 professional presets (gradients, padding, rounded corners, shadow, macOS window or browser frame).
- Every screenshot also lands in your clipboard history.
- Uploads are **opt-in** to a provider you choose (your own HTTPS endpoint, Imgur with your Client-ID, or 0x0.st).
  Links are public to anyone who has them. DittoMac does not use Lightshot's prnt.sc service.

### Locked sticky notes
Lock any clip or note so it can't be deleted by mistake and is encrypted on disk. Unlock with **Touch ID / your Mac
password** or a **new DittoMac password**. New Sticky Note (⌥⌘N) and floating desktop notes.

### Cloud sync and backup
Pick your **Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive** folder and DittoMac keeps an end-to-end encrypted copy of
your history there and merges it across your Macs. No account or token needed. Locked notes are never synced.

### Appearance
Light/dark/system, accent colour, custom themes, row density, timestamps, source-app icons, translucent popup,
menu-bar icon styles.

### Good to know
- **Screen Recording permission** is needed for screenshots (System Settings → Privacy & Security). DittoMac is ad-hoc
  signed, so macOS asks again after each update. Accessibility is still needed for auto-paste.
- Deleting a clip does not delete it from other Macs or backups yet.

Install: download **`DittoMac-1.2.0-universal.dmg`**, drag it to Applications and replace the old copy.
Universal build (Apple silicon + Intel), macOS 13+. Verify: `shasum -a 256 -c DittoMac-1.2.0-universal.dmg.sha256`
